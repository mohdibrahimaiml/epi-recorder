Privacy

Privacy Policy

The open-source tool runs on your machine and sends us nothing. The website and EPI Cloud collect only what they need to answer you and run the service.

LAST UPDATED: 5 NOVEMBER 2026

Who we are

EPI Labs is run by its founder, Mohd Ibrahim Afridi, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: mohdibrahim@epilabs.org. See About.

The open-source tool (epi-recorder)

  • Recording, sealing and checking happen on your machine. Your .epi files are not sent to us.
  • Usage telemetry is off by default. Nothing is sent unless you turn it on yourself (EPI_TELEMETRY_OPT_IN).
  • When sealing, the tool asks a public timestamp service (by default freetsa.org, then DigiCert) to timestamp the file. Only a hash of the record is sent, never its contents. Turn this off with EPI_NOTARIZE=0.
  • If you set up a witness or EPI Cloud, a hash-only fingerprint of each sealed record goes to the log you configured.

Checking a file in your browser

The check at /verify runs in your browser tab. The file you drop in is not uploaded.

The website

The website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our host (Cloudflare) keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

The contact form

When you send the form on Plans, we receive your name, work email, company and message, and use them only to reply and to discuss the engagement you asked about. The form is processed by our server on Render. We keep messages while we are in contact with you and for up to 24 months after, then delete them. If the form fails, it opens your own email program instead.

EPI Cloud (private beta)

If you sign in to cloud.epilabs.org, we store your account details from your sign-in provider (your name, email and account ID), your workspace, the records you choose to upload or witness, and the share links you create. We use them only to run the service for you. Hosting is on Cloudflare.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on your records.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email mohdibrahim@epilabs.org; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change here.
