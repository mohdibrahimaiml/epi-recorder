Straight answers

Why is verification free forever?

Cryptographic evidence only works if it's trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn't be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed. That's the spine of the company.

What do I get in a $1,500 sprint?

Five focused days with the founder: one agent workflow instrumented end-to-end, sealed artifacts your team can re-verify offline, and a short findings report. Priced against unblocking enterprise deals — not billable hours.

Is there a free trial?

No self-serve trial while EPI Cloud is in private beta — the retired Hosted/Team checkout is closed. Sprints are fixed-price and Design Partner places are scoped by hand.

What is a design partner pilot?

One of 5 private-beta places: hosted witness inbox, customer trust page, and Evidence Room for selected runs — with full retention in your bucket. Founding price locked, direct roadmap input, engagements scoped by hand.

What is EPI Cloud?

Witness-first evidence hosting: we witness the hash of every run and serve a trust page your customer verifies in their own browser. We hold no customer data — full .epi files stay in your bucket.
