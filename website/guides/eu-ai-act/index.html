EU AI Act Evidence Guide

Using .epi artifacts for EU AI Act evidence prep

High-risk AI workflows need durable records of what ran, when it happened, which controls fired, and who approved an action. EPI packages captured execution into a portable, Ed25519-signed .epi file verifiable offline.

Evidence input, not legal advice. EPI does not guarantee EU AI Act compliance, regulator approval, or conformity. Annex IV templates live in docs/ANNEX-IV.md as templates you fill with legal counsel.

What .epi evidence provides

  • Article 12 logging support: hash-chained steps.jsonl timelines with timestamps, inputs, tool calls, model identifiers, and sealed SHA-256 manifests.
  • Article 14 human oversight support: pre-action approval requests signed by a pinned approver key before the action executes, plus optional customer witness receipts showing request < response < executed order.
  • Independent inspection: offline CLI checks (epi verify) and zero-upload browser verification at epilabs.org/verify/.
  • Microsoft AGT import: package existing AGT exports via epi import agt ./agt-export --out agt-case.epi with a preserved mapping_report.json.

What .epi does not prove

  • It does not prove every uninstrumented model or tool call was captured.
  • It does not prove the decision was lawful, fair, or safe—or that a human approver read the underlying case deeply enough.
  • See Known limitations and the full Markdown guide.
