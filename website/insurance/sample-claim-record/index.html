Why it looks like that

The record is a document, not a web page.

The record keeps its own paper style on purpose. It is the thing a carrier attaches to a claim file or hands to an examiner, and it has to read the same whether it is opened today, printed, or read five years from now. Dressing it in the marketing site's theme would tie the evidence to a brand that can change.

This page is the wrapper: site navigation, context and the downloads. What sits inside the frame is the artifact itself. The same applies to the Decision Record and its sample.
