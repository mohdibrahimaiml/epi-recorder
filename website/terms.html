What a record does and does not prove

A valid seal shows a record has not changed since it was sealed and, once you have pinned the sender's key, who sealed it. It does not prove that everything that happened was captured, and it is not a compliance certificate. EPI does not make anyone compliant with any law or standard. See Known limitations.
