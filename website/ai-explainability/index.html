AI Explainability

Decision-level AI explainability you can hand to a reviewer

When an insurer denies a claim or a lender declines credit, regulators and customers do not ask for attention heatmaps—they ask for the inputs, policy rules, principal reasons, model version, and human override recorded at the moment the decision happened.

Read the Decision Record spec Inspect a sample decision

Operational AI explainability vs. black-box research

In production AI agents, explainability breaks when the evidence disappears. Prompts change weekly, retrieved documents expire, models deprecate, and staff override recommendations in separate ticketing tools. Without a sealed record, an explanation written months later looks like post-hoc rationalization.

What a .epi Decision Record preserves

  • Exact context seen by the model: retrieved evidence, tool calls, and tool responses (with secrets and sensitive patterns redacted by default).
  • Policy & rule evaluation: the exact rulebook (policy.json) and control outcomes (policy_evaluation.json) active during the run.
  • Model provenance: provider, model ID, parameters, and timestamps tied to the run.
  • Human-in-the-loop sign-off: cryptographic approver signatures showing who reviewed, approved, or overrode the AI recommendation.

Honest boundaries

EPI records and seals the reasoning, inputs, and approvals your application emits—it does not inspect model weights or guarantee that an explanation satisfies a specific statutory standard on its own. For formal reviews, pair EPI with our 10-question completeness checklist and standards guidance.
