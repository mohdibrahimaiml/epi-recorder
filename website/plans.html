Questions

What buyers ask before a call.

What is a customer relationship?

One named customer — a bank, insurer, carrier or auditor — whose runs, witness and reviewer link are kept separate. Agents are unlimited on every plan; we never charge per agent.

What counts as a file?

One sealed .epi decision record in the Evidence Room. Free keeps 10 a year, Startup 1,000, Growth 10,000. Recording and verifying offline are unlimited and never counted.

Can I self-host?

Yes. The recorder, witness receiver and verifier run on your infrastructure; Cloud is optional. The paid Enterprise package adds the operational layer: gateway, adapters, guardrails, private deployment and a licence issued to your organisation.

What happens after cancellation?

Retention lasts while the subscription is active. After cancelling you have 90 days to export everything. The free workspace keeps 30 days of storage.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — mutual, on your paper or ours. We can review a customer MSA too; if we cannot accept a term, we say so before the engagement starts.

What do you need from me?

One workflow, a test case that looks like a real decision, access to the agent's code and configuration, and a named reviewer on the customer side.
