Straight answers

Why is verification never charged?

A reviewer must be able to check a file without paying EPI Labs or logging into our dashboard. The CLI is MIT-licensed. That is the spine of the company.

What do I get in a $1,500 sprint?

Five focused days with the founder: one agent workflow instrumented end-to-end, sealed artifacts your team can re-verify offline, and a short findings report. Priced against unblocking enterprise deals — not billable hours.

Is there a free trial of Cloud?

Cloud is private beta, not a self-serve checkout. Open-source recording and verification do not need a trial. The $1,500 sprint is billed at purchase (Paddle); refunds follow the refund page.

What is the Design Partner Program?

Five places. Founding price locked when you join (set in writing — not a public menu). The founder instruments one workflow. In return: feedback calls, and a logo or case study only if the partner approves.

How working together works

01 · Talk

A 30-minute call — or just email. You describe the agent workflow and who needs to be convinced (auditor, insurer, regulator). We say honestly whether EPI fits. If it doesn't, we'll tell you.

02 · Sprint or design partner

A $1,500 sprint (one workflow, five days, files you keep) or a Design Partner place (founding price locked when you join). Cloud is private beta — not a checkout page.

03 · Prove it internally

Your team verifies with open tools — no dashboard login required to trust a seal. If your auditor can't reproduce it, we haven't finished the job.

04 · Scale

Design partners keep founding terms. Cloud self-serve plans come after beta; verification stays unmetered.
