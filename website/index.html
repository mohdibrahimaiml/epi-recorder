Step 01 · Capture

Record it

Wrap OpenAI, Anthropic, LangChain, LiteLLM, or route through epi gateway serve. Every step is hash-chained as it executes, with secrets redacted by default.

Step 02 · Cryptographic Lock

Seal it

At run close, EPI packs an Envelope v2 polyglot (HTML + ZIP), signs the canonical manifest with Ed25519, and attaches an optional RFC 3161 timestamp.

Step 03 · Independent Check

Prove it

Auditors, underwriters, and bank reviewers check the file offline via epi verify --policy strict or inside a browser tab. Verification is MIT-licensed and free forever.

02 · Cryptographic Separation

Seal, identity, and policy are three different questions.

Marketing that collapses integrity, identity, and compliance into one green badge is dishonest. EPI separates them in code: a valid seal proves bytes weren’t altered, epi keys trust pins who signed, and epi analyze grades policy rules.

04 · EPI Cloud Witness (Private Beta)

Independent hash-only witness receipts without uploading your payloads.

Full .epi records stay in your bucket. When a run seals, only its 32-byte SHA-256 digest is sent to the Witness log, which returns an Ed25519 receipt and signs a daily checkpoint at 00:05 UTC. Share selected runs with a reviewer via a fragment-protected trust link (/t/{slug}#k=…).

FAQ

  • EPI (Evidence Packaged Infrastructure) is the open-source evidence layer for AI agents. It records agent executions, seals them into a portable .epi Envelope v2 container signed with Ed25519, and lets any third party verify the record offline without an account or server.

  • A sealed .epi proves that after sealing, none of the member files or outer viewer HTML were altered, and the canonical manifest was signed by the Ed25519 private key matching the embedded public key. It does not by itself prove that every real-world call was captured, or who the signer is until you pin their key out of band (epi keys trust).

  • Because anyone can generate a fresh keypair and sign a file. A valid signature proves internal consistency, not identity. Until an auditor pins the vendor’s key fingerprint out of band with epi keys trust and runs epi verify --policy strict, EPI honestly reports NOT_PINNED (amber) rather than a misleading green checkmark.

  • Observability dashboards are hosted by the party being audited and require a login. EPI produces a portable, cryptographically sealed file that leaves your infrastructure and can be verified offline five years later by an insurer, bank reviewer, or regulator with open-source MIT tooling.

  • Run pip install epi-recorder && epi demo --no-browser to record, seal, and verify your first artifact offline in 60 seconds — or apply for one of the five Design Partner places to have the founder instrument a consequential workflow with you.
