What happens next

01 · Receipt

Paddle emails your receipt within minutes — check spam if you don't see it. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the link in that email (customer portal).

02 · Onboarding

You'll receive a short setup guide for your tier. If anything in it doesn't match what you need, reply to the email — a human (the founder) reads it.

03 · Seal your first run

The CLI is the product: pip install epi-recorder, wrap your agent, and verify the sealed file offline. Everything else builds on that loop.
