Decision Record

Every AI decision needs a Decision Record.

One readable record per decision: what the model saw, what it decided and why, which model answered, who approved it, and what happened next. Not a log. Not a trace. Not a dashboard.

Open a real Decision Record Download the checklist

No account · opens in the browser · the checklist is a Markdown one-pager

Why not a log?

Logs are written for the system. Decision Records are written for the person who has to answer for the decision.

What you have Who it serves The question it answers What it cannot do
Logs Engineers What did the system do? Explain one decision to a customer
Traces Engineers Where did time and tokens go? Show reasons and approval per case
Governance dashboards Risk and compliance Do we have the policy and the inventory? Produce the record of a specific decision
Decision Record The customer, reviewer, auditor Why was this case decided this way, and who approved it? Replace your observability stack

What is inside

  • The goal and the case. What the agent was asked to do, for whom.
  • The steps. What the model saw, which tools it called, what came back.
  • The decision. The outcome recorded in plain language, with the reasons.
  • The rules. The policy, limit or eligibility rule that applied.
  • The model. Which model and version answered, and when.
  • The approval. Who signed off, with which key, and when — or that none was required.
  • The outcome. What happened next: the action taken or the customer result.

The ten-question completeness checklist

Score one point per question you can answer from the record itself, without asking the vendor to log in or search another system.

  1. What was decided, in plain language?
  2. What outcome was recorded, and when?
  3. What inputs and data did the model rely on?
  4. Which model and version answered?
  5. What rules, policy or limit applied?
  6. What reasons were recorded for the decision?
  7. Who or what approved it, and when?
  8. If a human approval was required, is it attached to this record?
  9. What happened next — the action taken or the customer outcome?
  10. Can the customer or reviewer read this record without the vendor's system?

9–10: a Decision Record. 6–8: close, but reasons, approval or outcome are incomplete. 3–5: observability data. 0–2: logs.
