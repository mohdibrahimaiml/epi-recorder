Straight answers

Why is verification free forever?

Cryptographic evidence only works if it's trustless. If you had to pay EPI Labs to verify your own files, it wouldn't be trustless. The CLI is MIT-licensed. That's the spine of the company.

What do I get in a $1,500 sprint?

Five focused days with the founder: one agent workflow instrumented end-to-end, sealed artifacts your team can re-verify offline, and a short findings report. Priced against unblocking enterprise deals — not billable hours.

Is there a free trial?

No trial — you are charged at checkout. Cancel anytime with one click in your account portal; Paddle handles pro-rated refunds per their Merchant of Record terms.

What is a design partner pilot?

Someone whose needs go beyond standard tiers — org keys, hosted receipts, remote SCITT anchoring, or special procurement requirements. Founding pricing, direct roadmap input, engagements from $5K.
