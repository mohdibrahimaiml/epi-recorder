Legal · Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we never do.

The open-source tool runs on your machine and sends us nothing. The website and EPI Cloud collect only what they need to answer you and run the service.

Who we are

EPI Labs is run by its founder, Mohd Ibrahim Afridi, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: mohdibrahim@epilabs.org. See About.

The open-source tool (epi-recorder)

  • Recording, sealing and checking happen on your machine. Your .epi files are not sent to us.
  • Usage telemetry is off by default. Nothing is sent unless you turn it on yourself (EPI_TELEMETRY_OPT_IN). If you do, EPI Cloud keeps only coarse fields (command, success, EPI and Python version, operating system, a random install ID) for 12 months, then deletes them. epi join-pilot sends the email and details you type, with your consent, to our contact inbox (kept 12 months, and emailed to the founder through Resend).
  • When sealing, the tool asks a public timestamp service (by default freetsa.org, then DigiCert) to timestamp the file. Only a hash of the record is sent, never its contents. Turn this off with EPI_NOTARIZE=0.
  • If you set up a witness or EPI Cloud, a hash-only fingerprint of each sealed record goes to the log you configured.

Checking a file in your browser

The check at /verify runs in your browser tab. The file you drop in is not uploaded.

The website

The website uses no advertising and no tracking cookies. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and counts page views without identifying you. Our host (Cloudflare) also keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and reliability.

The contact form

When you send the form on Plans, we receive your name, work email, company, what you are interested in and your message, and use them only to reply and to discuss the engagement you asked about. The form is stored by EPI Cloud on Cloudflare, and a copy is emailed to the founder’s inbox through Resend, our email provider, so we can reply quickly. We also store a salted hash of your IP address, never the address itself, to stop spam. We delete form messages 12 months after they arrive. If the form cannot be sent, it opens your own email program instead and says so.

EPI Cloud

If you sign in to cloud.epilabs.org, we store your account details from your sign-in provider (your name, email and account ID), your workspace, the records you choose to upload or witness, and the share links you create. We use them only to run the service for you. Hosting is on Cloudflare.

Payments

Card payments for EPI Cloud subscriptions are handled by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes your payment details and billing address under its own privacy policy; we never see or store your full card number. We receive from Paddle what we need to run your plan: the customer and subscription ids, your plan, billing email and payment status.

What we never do

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on your records.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email mohdibrahim@epilabs.org; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and describe the change here.
