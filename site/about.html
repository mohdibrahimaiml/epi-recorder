About

About EPI Labs

We build decision records for AI agents: one signed file per decision, which the vendor’s customer can check for themselves.

Why it exists

AI companies now sell decision-making agents to insurers, lenders and banks. Sooner or later the customer asks: what did your AI decide in this case, why, which model answered, and who approved it? And can we check that the answer was not rewritten afterwards? Logs and dashboards are kept by the vendor, so they cannot settle that. A sealed record that the customer keeps a copy of can.

How we work

  • Open source first. Recording, sealing and checking are Apache-2.0 from 4.10.0 and free (4.9.0 and earlier are MIT). Your customer never needs an account with us.
  • Honest limits. A seal proves a record has not changed and who sealed it. It does not prove everything was captured, and it does not make anyone compliant. We publish our known limitations.
  • Checkable releases. Releases are published to PyPI through Trusted Publishing, with build attestations and signed tags.

Standards work

EPI Recorder is listed as a community implementation in the AAIF TRACE specification proposal, and independent JCS conformance vectors cover EPI manifests (AlgoVoi). See Standards.
