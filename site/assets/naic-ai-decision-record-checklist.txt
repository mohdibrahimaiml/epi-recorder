# The minimum AI decision record

Ten questions for an insurance AI vendor, per decision

Pick one real decision the AI touched. Answer from the record itself, not from what the vendor could assemble for an exam. Every no is a gap.

1. For one specific decision, can you show what data the AI saw?
   Field: Inputs

2. Can you show what it recommended or decided, and why?
   Field: Decision and reasons

3. Which policy provisions or rules did it rely on, and which version was in force that day?
   Field: Policy and version

4. Which model and version answered, and would the record show if it changed later?
   Field: Model and version

5. Who reviewed it, and did they agree, change or override the AI?
   Field: Human review

6. When did each step happen?
   Field: Time

7. Was the record made at the time of the decision, not assembled later for an exam?
   Field: Contemporaneous

8. Can the carrier open it without logging into the vendor's system?
   Field: Portable

9. Would the carrier notice if the record were changed after it was handed over?
   Field: Tamper-evident

10. Can you list decisions that should have a record but don't?
   Field: Coverage

## How to score

One point per question you can answer from the record itself.

| Score | What it means |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | A decision record a carrier can rely on. |
| 6-8  | Close. Reasons, review or timing are incomplete. |
| 3-5  | Observability data. You can debug it; a carrier cannot read it. |
| 0-2  | Logs. The decision has to be reconstructed after the fact. |

Every no is a gap. The readiness assessment closes them.
