Refund Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who processes card payments

Card payments for EPI Cloud subscriptions are processed by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for those orders. Paddle handles payment, invoicing and sales tax, and issues any refund to your original payment method. Paid engagements and Enterprise agreements are invoiced directly by EPI Labs.

EPI Cloud Startup and Growth

  • 14-day refund on your first payment. If EPI Cloud is not right for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment for a monthly or annual plan and we refund it in full.
  • Renewals. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each monthly or annual period. Renewal payments are not refundable, except where the law requires it.
  • Cancel any time. Cancel from Manage billing in your EPI Cloud dashboard, or by email. Cancellation stops future renewals; your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • Changing plans. Upgrading from Free takes effect as soon as payment clears. To move between Startup and Growth, write to us; an upgrade is switched the same day and a downgrade takes effect at your next renewal.

Free workspace and open source

The free EPI Cloud workspace and the open-source epi-recorder CLI and Python SDK cost nothing, so no refund applies. The open-source tool is Apache-2.0 (4.10.0 and later; 4.9.0 and earlier are MIT).

Paid engagements

The readiness assessment and the full engagement are invoiced in full at the start, as described on Plans. Their cancellation and refund terms are the ones set out in your written order.

Enterprise agreements

Enterprise refund terms are defined in your individual enterprise agreement.

Retired Hosted and Team plans

If you bought one of the retired Hosted or Team subscriptions, the 14-day refund window above still applies to that purchase.

How to request a refund

Email mohdibrahim@epilabs.org with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the email address on your account, your plan, and the date of payment. You can also contact Paddle directly through the link on your payment receipt. Refunds are normally processed within 5 business days and returned to the original payment method.

Contact

For refund or billing questions: mohdibrahim@epilabs.org
